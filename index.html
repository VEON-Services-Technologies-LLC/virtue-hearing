Common Questions

Hearing Care Questions,
Answered

Is the hearing consultation really free?

Yes. There is no credit card, no obligation and no sales pressure. You meet with Christopher Medina at our Montebello office for about 30 minutes, get personalized recommendations for your hearing health, and receive Medicare and insurance information.

Do you accept Medicare and insurance?

Yes. We accept Medicare, MediCal and 50+ major insurance carriers, and our team handles the paperwork so you don't have to.

Do you offer financing for hearing aids?

Yes. We offer special financing with no interest for up to 24 months, CareCredit promotional financing on purchases of $200 or more, and in-house financing for patients who don't qualify for traditional credit. Financing is subject to credit approval.

Where is Virtue Hearing Aid Center and what are your hours?

We are at 134 S Montebello Blvd, Montebello, CA 90640. Hours are Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm, with weekend appointments available by request. Call or text (323) 530-0223.

Who will I see at my appointment?

You will meet Christopher Medina, a second-generation hearing aid specialist and the founder of Virtue Hearing Aid Center. Every consultation is unhurried and focused on listening to your needs.

Which hearing aid brands do you carry?

We partner with leading manufacturers including Starkey, Oticon, Signia, ReSound, Phonak, Unitron and Widex, so recommendations are matched to your hearing and lifestyle.

Can I check my hearing before I come in?

Yes. We offer an accessible online hearing test you can take from home with no appointment needed. For a full evaluation, book a free in-office hearing test with Christopher.

How do I book a free hearing test?

Use the form on this page, book online at virtuehearing.com/book.html, or call (323) 530-0223. We confirm every request within one business day.
